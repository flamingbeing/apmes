Rating scale (items 1–9)

Each item is rated on the following scale (one choice):

Not CharacteristicSlightly CharacteristicSomewhat CharacteristicMostly CharacteristicHighly CharacteristicNot Assessed

Professionalism

  1. Is conscientious, reliable, punctual, takes personal responsibility for lapses and seeks help to resolve complex interpersonal situations*
  2. Exhibits professional and ethical behaviour towards :
    • Fellow healthcare workers: is respectful and looks out for other team members
    • Patients and their families: maintains patient confidentiality*

Interpersonal & Communication Skills

  1. Patient- and Family-Centered Communication
    Communicates effectively and sensitively with patients and their families; effectively manages conflict*
  2. Develops/Maintains accurate medical records and communicates care of patients appropriately within the healthcare system*
  3. Uses close loop communication to verify understanding; maintains effective communication during crisis situation; manages feedback effectively for self and others*

Systems-Based Practice

  1. Patient Safety and Quality Improvement
    Ensures patient safety and is committed to improving quality of service*
  2. System Navigation for patient-centered care
    Coordinates care effectively for patients with other health care professionals and performs safe and effective transitions of care within and across healthcare delivery systems*

Practice-Based Learning

  1. Evidence-based and Informed Practice
    • Keeps up-to-date with knowledge and skills
    • Uses evidence based medicine to guide individualized patient care*
  2. Reflective Practice and Commitment to Personal Growth
    Is reflective, accepts and asks for feedback, and self-directed in seeking improvement*

Rating scale

Each item in the Professionalism, Interpersonal & Communication Skills, Medical Knowledge, Practice-Based Learning, Patient Care and Systems-Based Practice sections is rated on the following scale (one choice):

Not CharacteristicSlightly CharacteristicSomewhat CharacteristicMostly CharacteristicHighly CharacteristicNot Assessed

Professionalism

  • Shows Initiative*
  • Accepts responsibility and follows through on tasks*
  • Shows compassion and is respectful of patient's needs*
  • Demonstrates integrity and ethical behaviour*
  • Knows own limits and consults appropriately*
  • Works well with other healthcare professionals*
  • Copes well in stressful clinical situations*

Interpersonal & Communication Skills

  • Communicates effectively with patient and family*
  • Communicates effectively with other healthcare professionals*

Medical Knowledge

  • Possesses medical knowledge expected of the level of residency training*
  • Demonstrates a logical and analytical thought process in decision making*

Practice-Based Learning

  • Demonstrates self-directed/reflective learning*
  • Asks/seeks knowledge and guidance from faculty appropriately*

Patient Care

  • Demonstrates technical skills expected of the level of residency training*
  • Performs comprehensive assessment of patient*
  • Provides appropriate clinical care of patient*
  • Demonstrates the ability to plan and prepare for patient care*
  • Demonstrates the ability to prioritize tasks*
  • Possesses situational awareness and responds appropriately to urgent clinical situations*

Systems-Based Practice

  • Effectively coordinates patient care with healthcare professionals*
  • Demonstrates patient safety principles*
